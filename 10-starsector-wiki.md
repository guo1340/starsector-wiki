# 10-starsector-wiki.md

# Starsector Wiki Expansion Plan

## Project Goal

Build the most useful strategy-focused Starsector wiki for players.

Avoid creating a database clone.

Google already understands:

- ship stats
- weapon stats
- hull values

Our goal is to explain:

- what players should choose
- why they should choose it
- when choices become good or bad
- common mistakes
- faction strengths and weaknesses

The site should feel like advice from an experienced fleet commander.

---

# Core Audience

Primary audience:

- new players
- returning players
- players struggling with colony management
- players looking for fleet optimization

Secondary audience:

- modded players
- experienced players seeking optimization
